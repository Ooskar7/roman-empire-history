# Fall of the Western Roman Empire (476 AD)

## Overview

The fall of the Western Roman Empire is traditionally marked by the deposition of the last emperor, Romulus Augustulus.

## Historical Context

The empire had been weakening due to economic problems, military pressure, and political instability.

## Main Event

In 476 AD, the Germanic leader Odoacer deposed Romulus Augustulus and declared himself ruler of Italy.

## Consequences

- End of centralized Roman authority in the West
- Rise of barbarian kingdoms

## Legacy

This event is often considered the end of Ancient Rome and the beginning of the Middle Ages in Western Europe.
