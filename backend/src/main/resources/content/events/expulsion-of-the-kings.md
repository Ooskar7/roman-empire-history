# Expulsion of the Kings (509 BC)

## Overview

The expulsion of the last Roman king, Tarquinius Superbus, marked the end of the Roman monarchy and the beginning of the Roman Republic.

## Historical Context

Rome had been ruled by kings since its foundation. The later kings, especially Tarquinius Superbus, were seen as tyrannical and abusive.

## Main Event

The turning point came after the story of Lucretia, a noblewoman who was assaulted by the king’s son. Her death sparked outrage.

Led by Lucius Junius Brutus, the Roman nobles revolted and expelled the king from Rome.

## Consequences

- End of monarchy
- Creation of the Roman Republic
- Establishment of elected magistrates (consuls)

## Legacy

This event shaped Roman political identity. Romans strongly rejected kingship for centuries afterward.
