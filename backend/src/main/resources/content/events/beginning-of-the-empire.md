# Beginning of the Roman Empire (27 BC)

## Overview

The Roman Empire began when Octavian, later known as Augustus, became the first emperor.

## Historical Context

After decades of civil war, the Roman Republic was unstable. Julius Caesar’s assassination had created further chaos.

## Main Event

In 27 BC, Octavian was granted the title "Augustus" by the Senate.

Although he claimed to restore the Republic, he held ultimate power.

## Consequences

- End of the Republic
- Centralized authority
- Beginning of imperial rule

## Legacy

Augustus created a system that lasted for centuries and shaped the Roman Empire’s golden age.
