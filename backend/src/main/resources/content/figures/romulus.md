# Romulus

## Overview

Romulus is the legendary founder and first king of Rome.

## Early Life

According to myth, he and his twin brother Remus were abandoned and raised by a she-wolf.

## Founding of Rome

Romulus founded Rome in 753 BC after killing Remus in a dispute.

## Rule

He organized the city, created institutions, and established the Roman Senate.

## Legacy

Romulus became a symbol of Roman strength and origin. His story shaped Roman identity.
