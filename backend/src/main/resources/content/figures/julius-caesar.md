# Julius Caesar

## Overview

Julius Caesar was a military general and statesman who played a key role in the fall of the Roman Republic.

## Rise to Power

He gained fame through military campaigns, especially in Gaul.

## Dictatorship

In 49 BC, he crossed the Rubicon, starting a civil war.

He became dictator for life.

## Death

In 44 BC, he was assassinated by a group of senators.

## Legacy

His actions led to the end of the Republic and the rise of the Roman Empire.
