# Cicero

## Overview

Cicero was a Roman statesman, orator, and philosopher who played a major role in the politics of the late Republic.

## Early Life

Born in 106 BC, Cicero rose to prominence through his skill in rhetoric and law.

## Political Career

He served as consul and was a strong defender of the Roman Republic and its institutions.

Cicero opposed figures like Julius Caesar and later Mark Antony.

## Philosophy and Writing

Cicero wrote extensively on politics, ethics, and philosophy, helping to transmit Greek ideas into Roman culture.

## Death

In 43 BC, during the power struggles after Caesar’s death, Cicero was executed on the orders of Mark Antony.

## Legacy

Cicero is remembered as one of the greatest speakers in history and a symbol of republican values.
