# Constantine I

## Overview

Constantine I, also known as Constantine the Great, was a Roman emperor who played a crucial role in the transformation of the Roman Empire.

## Early Life

Born around 272 AD, Constantine rose through the military ranks during a time of political instability.

## Rise to Power

He became emperor after a series of civil wars, most notably defeating Maxentius at the Battle of the Milvian Bridge in 312 AD.

## Religious Policy

Constantine is famous for legalizing Christianity through the Edict of Milan in 313 AD.

He supported the Church and played a key role in its early development.

## Reforms

- Founded the city of Constantinople
- Reorganized the empire
- Strengthened imperial authority

## Legacy

Constantine transformed the Roman Empire by aligning it with Christianity, shaping the future of Europe and Western civilization.
